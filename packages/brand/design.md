## Source hierarchy

1. This file.
2. [`src/mark.ts`](src/mark.ts) for the symbol's geometry, and [`css/tokens.css`](css/tokens.css) for colour, type, spacing, radius, shadow and motion.
3. The masters in [`source/`](source) and the icons in [`favicon/`](favicon), generated by [`scripts/build_masters.py`](scripts/build_masters.py). Nobody edits them by hand.
4. [`brand.json`](brand.json), the machine-readable summary of this file.
5. How the web app uses the tokens.

When two sources disagree, the higher one wins and the lower one gets fixed in the same change.

## Brand foundation

### Product truth

A holder locks Stock Tokens they'd sell at a higher price, a sell target, or USDG they'd spend at a lower one, a buy target, and picks that price on the week's ladder. Each week the target is paid that week's payment, skips the week when the bid is under the holder's least payment, or fills at exactly the holder's price when Friday's close passes it, settled in the actual token. It renews until it fills or the holder cancels. Underneath, each target is a fully collateralized weekly option, but the holder never has to work in options terms.

### Audience

People who already hold Stock Tokens or USDG on Robinhood Chain and already have a price in mind. They know what selling at a price means. They don't want to learn strikes, expiries or assignment.

### Promise

Pick the price you'd sell at. Get paid while you wait for it.

### Load-bearing idea

The held price. The week's prices form a ladder, and one rung on it belongs to the holder. That rung stays level while the weeks move around it. The symbol, the colour roles and the landing hero all come from this one picture.

### Personality

Calm, exact and plain-spoken. Level lines, prices on a fixed grid, numbers that line up. It explains what happens instead of selling a feeling.

### Reasons to believe

- Every order is fully collateralized. The NVDA or USDG sits in the contract, and there's no margin.
- Fills settle in the actual token.
- Prices come from the Chainlink NVDA/USD feed, and the ladder sits every $5 within 10% of the last close.
- A one-file fallback page cancels and withdraws through the holder's own wallet, with no Vayle server involved.
- Bids come from a maker the Vayle team operates, and the contract marks its address as team-operated.

### Avoid

- Trading-floor imagery: candlesticks, charts going up, rockets, bulls and bears.
- Options vocabulary in anything a holder reads. The product says price, week and fill.
- Hype, any wording that makes the weekly payment sound guaranteed, and any APY or annualized figure.
- Blue on everything. Blue has two jobs, listed under Colour.
- The chain's or the token issuer's logos, marks or brand colours used as Vayle decoration.
- Glows, glass effects, neon on black, and purple-to-blue gradient washes.

## Visual identity

### Concept territory

The price ladder: the week's grid of prices, and the one rung that's yours.

### Logo construction

The symbol sits on a 48-unit grid. Its own bounds are x 8 to 45 and y 3 to 45, published as `MARK_BOUNDS_VIEWBOX` (`8 3 37 42`).

- Two rails, 4 units wide with round ends: x 8 to 12 and x 24 to 28, from y 3 to y 45.
- Two plain rungs, 4 units high and 12 wide, at y 10 to 14 and y 34 to 38.
- The held rung, 6 units high at y 21 to 27. It starts at the left rail, crosses the right rail and runs out to x 45, where it ends in a round cap of radius 3.
- Gaps between rungs are 7 units, and the rails run 7 units past the outer rungs, so the symbol is symmetric top to bottom about the held rung.
- Rails and plain rungs share one weight. At lockup scale, 4 units is 0.155 cap heights, which matches the wordmark's stems at weight 650. The held rung is one and a half times heavier.
- In colour, the ladder is ink and the held rung is brand blue. One-colour versions use `MARK_PATH`, the merged outline.

The wordmark is "Vayle" in Plus Jakarta Sans at weight 650 with -0.04 em tracking. HarfBuzz shapes it with the font's own kerning, and the glyphs are outlined, so no master depends on an installed font.

In the horizontal lockup, the symbol's bounds stand 1.86 cap heights tall and centre on the caps, and the gap before the word is a quarter of the symbol's height. In the stacked lockup, the symbol stands 2.6 cap heights tall over the word, 0.7 cap heights above it, and both are centred.

### Responsive marks

| Mark              | Use                                                                                | Source                                                              |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Horizontal lockup | Default wherever the name appears                                                  | `source/vayle-lockup-horizontal*.svg`                               |
| Stacked lockup    | Square spaces, such as avatars with room for the name                              | `source/vayle-lockup-stacked*.svg`                                  |
| Symbol            | 24 px and up, when the name is already on screen                                   | `source/vayle-symbol*.svg`, `MARK_LADDER_PATH` and `MARK_HELD_PATH` |
| Small symbol      | 16 and 32 px only. Redrawn on whole pixels: 1 px rails and rungs, a 2 px held rung | `MARK_SMALL_PATH`, `favicon/favicon.svg`                            |
| App icon          | The white symbol on brand blue, with a 22% corner radius and an 18% inset          | `source/vayle-app-icon.svg`                                         |

Platforms that cut their own shape get the app icon with square corners: `apple-touch-icon.png` and `icon-maskable-512.png`. The symbol stays inside the maskable safe circle.

### Clear space

Keep clear space on every side equal to half the symbol's height. In the app header the symbol is 27.7 px tall, so that's about 14 px.

### Minimum sizes

- Horizontal lockup: 64 px wide.
- Symbol: 24 px tall. Below that, use the small symbol.
- Small symbol: 16 px. Don't go smaller.

### Roles

Blue has two jobs.

- The holder's own price: the held rung in the symbol, the held line on the landing, the chosen row on the ladder, the "Your price" pill and the price on the order ticket.
- The next step: the one primary action in a view, links, and the stage the week is in now.

Bids, other prices and finished steps stay neutral. Ink is the default action. Green only means money paid to the holder, and orange-red only means something failed. Neither colour ever shows a price going up or down.

### Contrast and accessibility

Measured against WCAG 2.2 relative luminance:

| Pair                        | Light   | Dark    |
| --------------------------- | ------- | ------- |
| `--ink` on `--sheet`        | 20.23:1 | 17.05:1 |
| `--body` on `--sheet`       | 16.13:1 | 12.12:1 |
| `--muted` on `--sheet`      | 6.25:1  | 6.45:1  |
| `--brand-text` on `--sheet` | 7.07:1  | 5.27:1  |
| `--earned` on `--sheet`     | 5.69:1  | 9.50:1  |
| `--problem` on `--sheet`    | 5.52:1  | 8.32:1  |
| White on `--brand`          | 7.07:1  | 5.43:1  |
| `--ghost` on `--sheet`      | 3.19:1  | 3.63:1  |
| `--faint` on `--sheet`      | 2.89:1  | 3.27:1  |

`--ghost` passes only as large text, so it's for display headlines and nothing smaller. `--faint` doesn't pass as text in either theme, so it's for bars, rules and input placeholders.

Every theme keeps visible focus: a 2 px `--brand` outline. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Forbidden combinations

- `#253CEF` on the dark sheet (2.73:1). Dark mode uses `#6879FD` for blue text and the held rung.
- `#6879FD` on white or the light page (3.66:1 and 2.96:1).
- An ink held rung on the blue tile (2.86:1). On blue, the whole symbol is white.
- Green or red to show which way a price moved.
- Text in `--faint`.

## Typography

### Display

Plus Jakarta Sans at weight 400, with -0.035 em tracking and 1.02 leading. Display 1 runs from 44 to 72 px and display 2 from 38 to 64 px. A display headline can have two lines: a quiet line in `--ghost`, then the statement in `--ink`.

### Text

Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else. Headings use weight 600 with -0.025 em tracking, labels and buttons 560 to 650, and body text 400 with 1.55 leading. Figures use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so prices line up down the ladder.

### Mono

Not part of the brand. `--font-mono` is a system stack held in reserve for raw data such as transaction hashes, and no view uses it yet. Prices and figures stay in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Licensing and fallbacks

Plus Jakarta Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/plus-jakarta-sans` 5.3.0, so no page loads a font from a third party. The fallback stack is `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`.

## Layout

### Grid and spacing

Spacing runs on a 4 px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96 and 128 px, as `--s-1` to `--s-32`. Text aligns left. The landing centres only its hero and its one-line statement.

### Radius and borders

Radius steps down as surfaces nest: the sheet is 24 to 40 px, cards 28, inner panels 20, controls 12, chips 8, and buttons and pills are fully round. Surfaces separate with 1 px hairline rings or a change of surface colour, not heavy borders.

### Shadow and depth

Three shadows, tinted with ink in light mode and plain black in dark mode: `--shadow-xs` for controls, `--shadow-float` for cards that float over an illustration, and `--shadow-lift` for the hero's ladder and for toasts. Nothing else casts a shadow.

### Responsive behaviour

The landing reflows at 1080 px and 720 px, and the app at 1100 px and 640 px. Below 1100 px the app drops its sidebar and carries the lockup in the top bar, and below 640 px the top bar shows the symbol alone. On narrow screens the order ticket scrolls into view when a price is chosen. Neither page scrolls sideways at 320 px.

## Iconography

### Grid

UI icons are drawn on an 18-unit grid and sit in an 18 px box.

### Stroke and fill

Icons are 1.5-unit strokes with round caps and joins and no fill, in the text colour of their label. The symbol is the only filled mark. Tokens are ticker badges, not artwork.

### Perspective

Flat and front-on. No isometric or 3D icons.

### Reuse rules

- The symbol is never an icon, a bullet or a loading indicator, and no other icon draws a ladder.
- Every token is a ticker badge, from [`css/ticker.css`](css/ticker.css): its ticker in Plus Jakarta Sans at weight 700 with 0.02 em tracking, set at 0.44 of the badge's height and never under 10 px. A Stock Token is an ink tile with inverse text and corners at 0.3 of the height. USDG is a pill on the sheet with ink text and a 1 px `--control` ring. A choice that isn't the current one, or a token that isn't listed, is the outlined tile. Heights run 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 and 56 px. The badge sits to the left of the text that names its token, and on a surface of fixed colour, like the dark band, it keeps its contrast by turning over.
- Badges stay neutral: blue is for the holder's price, and no company's or issuer's artwork appears anywhere.
- Robinhood Chain is named in words, next to a neutral glyph.

## Image and render system

Vayle uses no photography, 3D renders or generated images. The imagery is the product itself: the ladder, target states and ticker badges.

### Composition

Product objects on the sheet, with plenty of empty space and one held line crossing the composition at the held price.

### Camera

Front-on with no perspective. Floating cards and stickers on the landing tilt by 3 to 9 degrees, and nothing else does.

### Lighting

Flat. Depth comes only from the shadow tokens.

### Materials and finishes

Matte surfaces: a white sheet in light mode and a deep navy sheet in dark. No glass, glow or gloss.

### Background and shadows

The page colour frames a rounded sheet. Illustrations sit on a dotted grid in `--dot`. Shadows come only from the tokens.

### Reference roles

None. The system uses no outside reference imagery.

### Standing avoid list

- Stock photos of traders, phones showing charts, or coins.
- Candlesticks, rising arrows and rockets.
- 3D or glossy tokens.
- Purple-to-blue glows, neon and glass effects.
- The chain's or the token issuer's artwork.

## Validation

- `pnpm --filter @vayle/brand test` checks that every master and icon was generated from the current `src/mark.ts`, that no SVG carries text, scripts or outside references, and that the web app serves the same icons as this package.
- After changing `src/mark.ts` or the wordmark settings, rerun `scripts/build_masters.py`, then `pnpm --filter @vayle/brand social`.
- By eye before release: the favicon at 16 and 32 px on light and dark browser tabs, and both lockups in both themes.
