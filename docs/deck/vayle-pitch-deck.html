Your sell price should do more than wait.

Vayle turns a price target for a Stock Token into a chance to earn a weekly payment.

vayle.xyz
01
The problem

Waiting for your price pays nothing.

You own a token tied to a stock. You know the price where you would sell. A normal limit order waits for that price, but gives you nothing while it waits.

$0
Earned by a waiting limit order
02
The idea

Put your price to work.

Set the price where you would sell. A buyer can pay you for the right to buy at that price this week.

You keep the token unless the buyer uses that right after the week closes.

03
How it works

Three steps. One week.

01

Pick a price

Choose the token, amount, and price where you would sell.

02

Get an offer

If a buyer takes your target, you receive a payment up front.

03

Settle at close

After the week ends, the buyer may buy your tokens at your price.

No offer means no payment. Vayle also supports targets for buying with USDG.

04
What you see

Every target has a clear result.

Paid

A buyer takes the target. You receive the weekly payment.

Skipped

No buyer takes it. No payment, and your target carries over to next week.

Filled

The buyer acts after the close. Your tokens sell at your chosen price.

The payment is for giving the buyer a choice. It is not a promised yield.

05
Who it is for

Built for people who already have a price in mind.

First user

A Stock Token holder who would sell at a chosen price.

They want a simple way to set that price and see if a buyer will pay for the week.

Also supported

A USDG holder who wants to buy at a chosen price.

They can set a buy target and wait for a buyer to take the other side.

Technical strength

Real assets back each target.

Assets are heldThe seller's tokens, or the buyer's USDG, are locked before a target can go live.
Tokens moveIf the buyer acts, Vayle settles with the actual Stock Token and USDG.
There is a backupAnyone can use the public fallback path if the normal app or team service fails.

The contracts have automated tests; they have not had an outside audit.

10
Business model

Make money when a target gets paid.

Today: no platform fee

We keep the fee at zero while we test demand and the user experience.

Later: a small maker fee

The buyer who pays for a target could pay a fee of up to 5% of that payment. Raising it requires a time delay.

The team currently supplies the buying side. We need more capital and more buyers before this can serve many users.
